Each Question in your Routing form can be mapped to a **Object Property** to save their responses, as well as use it to send the data to third party integrations such as CRMs and Zapier.

For information on **Object Properties**, please take a look at our [**Introduction to Objects and Properties**](/account-administration/objects-properties/introduction-to-objects-and-properties) article.

---

## Mapping a Question to an Object Property

Each Routing form allows you to configure which Questions will be saved into a Contact field using the following steps:

1. Open the **Flow Builder Tab** of the Routing Form.
2. Select the **Question** you want to save to a Contact field.
3. Select **In the conversation and as a contact field** in the pane on the right.
4. Toggle on **Do More With This Answer**.
5. Select the **Object** and **Property** you want to map the question to.
6. Click **Save.**

Once completed, the responses to the mapped questions will be automatically saved in OnceHub and can be included in integrations with tools like Salesforce, Hubspot, or Zapier.
