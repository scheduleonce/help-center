Share your dedicated [**Phone Booking Number**](/product/booking-calendars/phone-booking/introduction-to-phone-booking) for instant, direct scheduling. Guests can simply call your dedicated line instead of using a web browser to schedule a meeting.

This service is especially useful for clients who are:

- **Busy or On-the-Go**: Allows for convenient scheduling during commutes or other activities when browsing is impractical.
- **Seeking Accessibility**: Offers an essential, accessible alternative to online booking for those who are visually impaired or have other limitations.

---

## How to Share Your Phone Booking Number

Sharing your [**Phone Booking Number**](/product/booking-calendars/phone-booking/how-to-assign-phone-number-to-your-booking-calendar-or-booking-hub) makes it easy for clients to find and book time with you.

To maximize meetings, make sure the number is visible everywhere your clients look:

- **Email Signature**: Add the phone number to your professional email signature.
- **Website**: Display the number prominently on your "Contact Us" page or in the website footer.
- **Social Media**: List the booking number in the bio section of all your social media profiles (e.g., Facebook, Instagram).
- **Online Directories**: Update your online listings (e.g., Google Business Profile, Yelp) with the dedicated booking number.
