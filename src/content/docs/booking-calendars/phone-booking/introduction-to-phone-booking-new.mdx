**Phone Booking** allows your guests to schedule a meeting or leave a message as a fallback option using natural language over a phone call. This is a **no-setup feature** that uses your Booking Calendar's **existing availability and settings** to create a conversational scheduling experience.

To see if Phone Booking fits your scheduling needs, take a look at our [**When to Use Phone Booking for Scheduling**](/when-to-use-phone-booking-for-scheduling/) article for more details.

* * *

## How Phone Booking Works

Each Booking Calendar or Booking Hub can have its own **dedicated phone number**, allowing guests to interact with the AI-driven Phone Booking feature and easily schedule meetings. 

For more information on how to get started, see our article on [**How to Generate a Phone Number for Your Booking Calendar**](/how-to-generate-a-phone-number-for-your-booking-calendar-or-booking-hub/).

## The Guest Experience

When a guest calls the dedicated phone number for your **Booking Calendar** or **Booking** **Hub**, the Phone Booking feature answers with your personalized welcome message. From there it begins a conversation with your guest by offering two options:

*   **Schedule a meeting:** Your guest will be guided through the process of booking a meeting.
*   **Leave a message:** Your guest will be able to leave a message for the host.

When a guest chooses to schedule a meeting, the Phone Booking feature will first schedule the meeting and then ask the free text questions that you have set up in your Booking Calendar’s Booking form.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

*   ### Is this feature available on all plans?
    
    No, the Phone Booking feature is only available on our paid plans.
*   ### Are there extra costs for my guests to use this service?
    
    No, but standard phone call rates may apply.
*   ### Are there extra costs for me as a user to use Phone Booking?
    
    During the early access phase, Phone Booking is offered at no extra charge. Future pricing may be introduced at a later stage.
*   ### Can I generate phone numbers for any country?
    
    Currently, automatic generation is limited to U.S. and Canada phone numbers. For all other countries, please [**contact our Sales team**](https://www.oncehub.com/contact?).
