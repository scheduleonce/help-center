**Workload Management** prevents team members from becoming overwhelmed by limiting the number of bookings they can receive per day, week, or month. This helps maintain productivity while ensuring a balanced workload.

## How Workload Management Works

*   **Set Booking Limits:** Specify the maximum number of bookings a team member can accept per day, week, or month.
    
*   **Independent Per Calendar:** Each **Booking Calendar** has its own separate booking limit. Bookings made with **Calendar A** will not affect the limit for **Calendar B**, even if the same host is used in both calendars.
    

* * *

## Where to Find the Setting

**To Manage Workload Limits:**

1.  Click **Booking Calendars** in the left-hand menu.
    
2.  Select the **Booking Calendar** you want to edit.
    
3.  Navigate to the **Host and Co-Hosts** section in the **Booking Settings Tab**.
    
4.  Click **Manage** next to **Workload** and adjust the limit as needed.
    

* * *

By setting workload limits, you can ensure that your team remains productive without becoming overburdened, creating a balanced and efficient scheduling process.
