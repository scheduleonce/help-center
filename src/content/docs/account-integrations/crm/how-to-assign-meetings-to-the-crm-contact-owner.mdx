You can override standard meeting distribution rules to have visitors schedule directly with their assigned contact owners. OnceHub prioritizes owners from your integrated [**Salesforce**](/account-integrations/crm/integrating-oncehub-with-salesforce/) or [**HubSpot**](/account-integrations/crm/integrating-oncehub-with-hubspot/) account, using the OnceHub contact owner as a fallback if no CRM is connected.

This is done by enabling the **Schedule with CRM Contact Owner** toggle located within the **Booking Calendar** action of your [**Routing Forms**](/product/routing-forms/introduction-to-routing-forms/introduction-to-routing-forms/).

---

## How Meeting Assignment Priority Works

When a visitor engages with a Routing Form, OnceHub identifies them by their provided email address. OnceHub then determines which team member should host the meeting based on a strict priority hierarchy:

- **CRM Contact Owner:** If a CRM is connected, OnceHub checks for an assigned contact owner. _(Note: If both Salesforce and HubSpot are connected at the same time, only Salesforce will be checked.)_ If a matching CRM contact owner is found and belongs to the [**Team selected as the host on your Booking Calendar**](/product/booking-calendars/booking-settings/booking-calendar-meeting-distribution-options/), the visitor will schedule with them.
- **No CRM Contact Owner Fallback (When CRM is connected):** If a CRM is connected but no matching CRM contact owner is found, there is no fallback to OnceHub contact owners. The system will immediately move to the **Standard Availability Fallback**.
- **OnceHub Contact Owner Fallback (When NO CRM is connected):** If no CRM is connected, the system checks its database for an existing OnceHub contact owner and schedules the meeting with them instead.
- **Standard Availability Fallback:** If no applicable contact owner exists (or if a connected CRM returns no match), the standard distribution rules set on the Booking Calendar will be used.

---

## How to Enable Contact Owner Meeting Assignment

Follow the steps below to enable this functionality in your Routing Form:

1. Click **Routing Forms** in the left-hand navigation menu.
2. Select the Routing Form you wish to edit.
3. Select the **Booking Calendar** action that will be used to schedule with contact owners.
4. Enable **Schedule with CRM Contact Owner**.
5. Click **Save** to apply the changes.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

**Q: How does OnceHub identify and match contacts?**

**A:** User matching is performed using the user's provided **email address** (and not their username).

**Q: What happens if both Salesforce and HubSpot are connected?**

**A:** If both integrations are active, OnceHub will check only **Salesforce** to determine contact ownership.

**Q: What happens if a connected CRM has no owner assigned for a contact?**

**A:** If a CRM is connected and no contact owner is found, the system does not fall back to OnceHub contact owners. It immediately falls back to standard Booking Calendar distribution rules.

**Q: What happens if the contact owner has no available time slots?**

**A:** If the contact owner has no open time slots, a message stating that no times are available will be displayed to the visitor while scheduling.

**Q: What happens if a contact has different owners assigned in OnceHub compared to the CRM?**

**A:** OnceHub prioritizes the contact owner listed in your integrated CRM to ensure your source-of-truth data remains accurate.
