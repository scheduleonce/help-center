Use the **Corporate Mailbox** feature to send OnceHub notifications from your organization's custom corporate email server or service instead of the default mailer@oncehub.com. This helps maintain compliance, allows control over outbound emails, and provides a professional experience for your guests and users.

<Aside type="note">
  Depending on your plan type, you may need to purchase the **Security and
  Compliance Add-On** to use this feature.
</Aside>

---

## Impact of Enabling the Corporate Mailbox Feature for Your Account

Once connected, all scheduling notifications sent to Guests and Hosts will originate from the designated corporate email server or service.

<Aside type="note">
  User-level email connections override the account-level Corporate Mailbox. If
  individual users have enabled personal email integrations, notifications for
  their bookings will be sent from their personal addresses instead.
</Aside>

---

## How to Set Up Corporate Mailbox

Follow these steps to connect your email server or service to OnceHub:

### 1. Accessing the Corporate Mailbox Settings

1. Click the gear icon located in the top-right corner of the page.
2. Select **Account Integrations** from the dropdown menu.
3. Filter for the **Corporate Mailbox** tab.
4. Click the **Custom SMTP Server** tile.
5. Click **Connect** to begin setup.

### 2. Providing Credentials and Server Settings

Enter your email server or service details in the setup prompt:

- **Sending Email Address:** Specify the address used to send notifications.
- **User Name and Password / OAuth Authentication:** Enter the credentials or authenticate with your corporate email service provider.
- **Sending Email Name Label:** Enter the display name that appears on recipient emails.
- **Server Host and Port (if applicable):** Enter your server host URL and port number (e.g., port 587 or 465).
- **Enable Encryption (if applicable):** Select this option if your server requires TLS/SSL encryption.

### 3. Finalizing the Setup

After entering all required credentials:

- Double-check your configuration settings for accuracy.
- Click **Connect** to complete the setup.

---

## Reconnection and Disconnection Handling

- **Reconnecting existing connections:** If an active email connection breaks, click **Reconnect** to update credentials or server settings.
- **Disconnection behavior:** Disconnecting an active email server or service reverts guest notification emails to individual host user settings.
- **Fallback on connection failure:** If an active connection encounters an issue, notifications will temporarily route from mailer@oncehub.com to prevent lost notifications. An alert email will be sent to Admins once every 24 hours until the connection is restored.
