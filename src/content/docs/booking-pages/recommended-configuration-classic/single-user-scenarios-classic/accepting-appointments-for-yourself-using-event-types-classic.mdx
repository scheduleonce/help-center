When you accept appointments for yourself, people come to your Booking page and book appointments with you [automatically or with your approval](/automatic-booking-or-booking-with-approval/ "Automatic Booking or Booking with Approval"). 

If your [Booking page is associated with Event types](/adding-event-types-to-booking-pages/ "Adding Event types to Booking pages"), take the following steps to customize your Event type for personal appointment scheduling. Remember to click the **Save** button after each step.

1.  Select the relevant Event type.
2.  Click [Scheduling options](/introduction-to-the-scheduling-options-section/ "Introduction to the Scheduling options section"). Select your preferred mode between [Automatic booking or Booking with approval](/automatic-booking-or-booking-with-approval/ "Automatic Booking or Booking with Approval").
3.  Click [Time slot settings](/introduction-to-time-slot-settings/ "Introduction to Time Slot Settings"). Set your preferred duration, [Time slot display](/time-slot-display/ "Time slot display"), [Workload rules](/workload-rules/ "Workload Rules"), [Timeframe rules](/timeframe-rules/ "Timeframe rules"), and other settings related to time slots.
4.  Click [Booking form and redirect](/introduction-to-the-booking-form-redirect-section/ "Introduction to the Booking form and redirect section").Select the Booking form that will be used to gather valuable information when your Customers book online.
5.  Click [Customer notifications](/introduction-to-customer-notifications/ "The Customer Notifications section"). Select which notifications your Customers will receive and by which delivery method (email or SMS).
6.  Click [Payment and cancel/reschedule policy](/event-type-payment-and-cancelreschedule-policy-section/ "Event type: Payment and cancel/reschedule policy section"). Select a **Payment and pricing** option and choose your cancellation and reschedule policies.
7.  Click [Public content](/event-type-public-content-section/ "Event type: Public content section") and update the event information.
8.  Review the rest of the Booking page settings for final adjustments.[](http://help.scheduleonce.com/customer/portal/articles/462180-introduction-to-booking-pages?b_id=7432)

You can create a quick test booking by going to the Booking page associated with this Event type. In the [Overview section](/booking-page-overview-section/ "Booking page: Overview section"), use the public booking link in the **Share & Publish** section to make sure that everything is exactly as you want it.
