Each Custom field that you create for your Booking Forms can be mapped to a Contact field to save the corresponding guest responses within a OnceHub Contact.

For information on creating Contact Fields, please take a look at our [**Contacts in OnceHub article**](/contacts-in-oncehub/).

* * *

## Mapping a Question to a Contact Field

Define which fields in a Booking Form should save to a Contact field, and these preferences will be carried over to all Booking Pages and Event Types using it.

1.  Open the **Booking forms editor** on the left hand panel.
2.  Expand **Custom fields** on the right hand side.
3.  Click the pencil icon of the custom field you want to save to a Contact field
4.  Select **In the booking and as a contact field**.
5.  Click on **Save**.
