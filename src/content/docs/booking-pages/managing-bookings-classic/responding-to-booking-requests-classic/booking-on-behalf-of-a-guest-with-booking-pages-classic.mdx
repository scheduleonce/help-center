To book on behalf of a guest you need to schedule a booking on your Booking/Master Page's link as if you are the guest and fill in their information.

### Opening the Booking/Master Page

1.  Click on **Booking Pages** in the left-hand navigation menu.
2.  Click on **Share** at the top-right.
3.  Select the Booking/Master page you want to use.
4.  Click on **Copy & close**.
5.  Paste the link in a new tab in your browser.

### Making the Booking

Go through all the steps in the Booking/Master page and fill in all of the guest's details in the booking form.
