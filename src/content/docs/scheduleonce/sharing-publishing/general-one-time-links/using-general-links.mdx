Each [Booking page](/scheduleonce/event-types-booking-pages/booking-pages/introduction-to-booking-pages "Introduction to Booking pages") and [Master page](/scheduleonce/master-pages-resource-pools/master-pages/introduction-to-master-pages "Introduction to Master pages") has its own unique Public link that can be shared with your prospects and Customers. The links that you share can either be general or [personalized](/scheduleonce/sharing-publishing/personalized-links/using-personalized-links "Using Personalized links").

In this article, you'll learn about using General links.

## Understanding General links

Using General links is the simplest way to share your pages. General links are standard links that you can share with Customers to allow them to schedule meetings with you. General links use the format "https://go.oncehub.com/" combined with a Public link name that you provide.

For example, a General link using the Public link name "Dana" would look like this: https://go.oncehub.com/dana.

<Aside type="note">

&#x20;[Booking pages](/scheduleonce/event-types-booking-pages/booking-pages/introduction-to-booking-pages "Introduction to Booking pages") only use HTTPS and automatically redirect to HTTPS if HTTP is manually typed by a User.

</Aside>

General links can be [sent by email](/scheduleonce/sharing-publishing/personalized-links/sharing-your-booking-links-in-emails-and-other-apps "Options for sharing your booking links in emails and other apps"), added to your email signature, or added to any other clickable element. When your Customer schedules a meeting using a General link, they will be required to identify themselves by [filling in their personal details](/scheduleonce/event-types-booking-pages/booking-forms-redirect/introduction-to-booking-form-and-redirect "Introduction to the Booking form and redirect section") in the [Booking form](/scheduleonce/customization-branding/booking-forms-editor/creating-and-editing-booking-form "Creating and editing a Booking form") step.

<Aside type="note">

If you want to prepopulate the Booking form with a Customer's details, or you want your Customer to skip the Booking form step altogether, you can also create a Personalized link.

When you send a Customer a Personalized link, all they have to do to schedule a meeting is pick a time. [Learn more about Personalized links](/scheduleonce/sharing-publishing/personalized-links/using-personalized-links "Using Personalized links")

</Aside>

## Accessing your link

You can access your booking links by following the steps below:

1. Click on **Booking Pages** in the left-hand menu.
2. Click on **Share** near the top-right.
3. Select the Booking page or Master page you want to share from the dropdown menu.
4. Click on **Copy & close** to save it to you clipboard.

You can also change the link for your Booking page or Master page in the page's Overview section. [Learn more about changing your Booking page or Master page link](/scheduleonce/sharing-publishing/general-one-time-links/changing-booking-page-and-master-page-links "Changing Booking and Master page links ")

<Aside type="tip">

You can use the [OnceHub for Gmail extension](/user-integrations/browser-extension/introduction-to-oncehub-for-gmail "Introduction to OnceHub for Gmail") to schedule with general links directly from your Gmail account. You can generate links, copy them in a single click, and send them in an email.

[Learn more about OnceHub for Gmail](/user-integrations/browser-extension/introduction-to-oncehub-for-gmail "Introduction to OnceHub for Gmail")

</Aside>
