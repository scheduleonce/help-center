The image gallery contains images that you can use for your [Event types](/scheduleonce/event-types-booking-pages/event-types/introduction-to-event-types "Introduction to Event types"), [Booking pages](/scheduleonce/event-types-booking-pages/booking-pages/introduction-to-booking-pages "Introduction to Booking pages"), and [Master pages](/scheduleonce/master-pages-resource-pools/master-pages/introduction-to-master-pages "Introduction to Master pages").

<Aside type="note">

All images in this gallery are property of OnceHub and are protected under copyright law. Use is permitted for your OnceHub Event types, Booking pages, and Master pages only.

</Aside>

## How to use the image gallery

1. To download an image in this article, right click it and select **Save image as...** from the menu.
2. Save the image on your computer.
3. Upload the image by clicking **Choose file** in the Public content section of an [Event type](/scheduleonce/event-types-booking-pages/event-types/public-content "Event type: Public content section"), [Booking page](/scheduleonce/event-types-booking-pages/booking-pages/booking-pages-public-content "Booking page: Public content section"), or [Master page](/scheduleonce/master-pages-resource-pools/master-pages/master-pages-public-content "Master Page: Public Content section") (Figure 1). ![](files/1562322354434.png)Figure 1: Event types Public content section
