The Theme designer allows you to fully customize the look and feel of your [Booking pages](/scheduleonce/event-types-booking-pages/booking-pages/introduction-to-booking-pages "Introduction to Booking pages") and [Master pages](/scheduleonce/master-pages-resource-pools/master-pages/introduction-to-master-pages "Introduction to Master Pages").

## Requirements

All OnceHub Administrators can access the Theme designer. You do not need an assigned product license to update the Theme designer, create custom themes, or update a Booking page's theme.

## Using themes

Go to **Booking pages** in the bar on the left → **Theme designer** on the left. [System themes](/scheduleonce/customization-branding/theme-designer/system-themes "System themes")

Themes allow you to comply with any design policy your organization may have. You can [create one theme](/scheduleonce/customization-branding/theme-designer/custom-themes "Custom Themes") and apply it to all of your pages to achieve uniform branding, or create multiple themes to make each of your pages unique. [Learn more about creating custom themes](/scheduleonce/customization-branding/theme-designer/custom-themes "Custom Themes")

You can also select different themes for the same Booking page or Master page when you [integrate it onto your website](/scheduleonce/sharing-publishing/publishing-on-websites/website-embed "Website embed"). This gives you the flexibility to visually fit a single Booking page on multiple different website pages.

All themes are centrally managed in the Theme designer. This means that any design changes you make are automatically implemented on the relevant Booking pages and Master pages.

## System themes and custom themes

The Theme designer comes with two [system themes](/scheduleonce/customization-branding/theme-designer/system-themes "System Themes") you can use as-is, or modify them to add your logo and change the background color.

If you need more customization for your theme, you can create a custom theme by duplicating an existing theme and modifying it, or you create a new custom theme from scratch. [Learn more about creating custom themes](/scheduleonce/customization-branding/theme-designer/custom-themes "Custom Themes")

You can preview a theme on a Booking page or Master page by selecting the **Preview** button (Figure 1).

## Theme properties

The specific design of a theme comes from the properties which you can define in four sections of the Theme designer:

- Core properties
- Page background
- Interaction pane
- Information pane

[Learn more about Theme properties](/scheduleonce/customization-branding/theme-designer/theme-properties "Theme properties")
