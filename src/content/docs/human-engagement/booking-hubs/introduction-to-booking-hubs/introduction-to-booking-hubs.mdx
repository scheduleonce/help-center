A **Booking Hub** combines multiple **Booking Calendars** into a single, easy-to-navigate interface for your visitors. This unified view presents options within a **hierarchical structure** that you define, giving visitors greater flexibility and ease in selecting their desired scheduling options.

Follow our guide on [**Grouping Booking Calendars with Booking Hubs**](/human-engagement/booking-hubs/configuring-bookng-hubs/how-to-group-booking-calendars-with-booking-hubs) if you would like to know how to create a Booking Hub.

---

## Enhanced Visitor Experience

Visitors can easily navigate the Booking Hub, following logical steps to find exactly what they need.

The Booking Hub serves as a unified page that allows visitors to choose from multiple [**Booking Calendars**](/human-engagement/booking-calendars/introduction-to-booking-calendars/introduction-to-booking-calendars), making the scheduling process more versatile and intuitive.

---

## Flexible Organization

**Booking Hubs** allow you to group **Booking Calendars** in two distinct ways to suit your business needs:

- **Organizing in a Flat List:** In a flat list, all options are displayed at once in a flat structure. This format gives you full control over the order and names of the calendars presented, ensuring that the most relevant options are prioritized for your visitors.
- **Organizing in a Hierarchical Structure:** For more complex setups, you can organize calendars into a multi-step hierarchical flow. This allows you to **customize the hierarchy** using **Categories**, guiding visitor through logical steps such as selecting &#x61;**&#x20;_Service Type_** followed by a **_Department_**.

---

## Using User Attributes with Booking Hub

**User Attributes** enhance the guest experience by allowing them to filter options based on host characteristics like **_Language_**,_&#x20;**Location**&#x20;_&#x6F;r **_Specialization_**. These User Attributes function seamlessly regardless of your organizational choice:

- **With Flat Lists:** Visitors can use User Attributes as a powerful **filtering tool** to narrow down a long list of available Booking Calendars to find the host that best meets their needs.
- **With Hierarchical Structures:** User Attributes work alongside **Categories** to further refine the search. A visitor can apply filters to ensure the final selection matches their specific requirements.

To learn more, read the [**Introduction to User Attributes** ](/account-administration/user-attributes/introduction-to-user-attributes)article.

## Customization

Booking Hubs are fully customizable to suit your branding and business flow. You can:

- Add images, social media links, and custom text.
- Create personalized backgrounds to reflect your brand identity.

Please take a look at [**this article**](/human-engagement/booking-calendars/sharing-embedding/how-to-design-page-for-your-booking-calendar) if you want to learn more about the customization options that are available.

## Flexible Sharing Options

Once your Booking Hub is ready, getting it in front of your guests is easy. You can:

- Share a direct link.
- Generate a QR code for quick access.
- Embed the hub directly on your website for a seamless guest experience.

To learn more, see our article on how to [**Share and Embed Your Booking Calendar/Hub**](/human-engagement/booking-calendars/sharing-embedding/share-and-embed-your-booking-calendar).
