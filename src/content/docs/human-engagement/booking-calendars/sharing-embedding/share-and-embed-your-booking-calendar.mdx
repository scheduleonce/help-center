Your **Booking Calendar** is a powerful tool for streamlining appointments, and there are two easy ways to share it with your customers:

- Embed it directly on your website for a cohesive, integrated experience.
- Share a standalone, fully branded **Booking Page** using the **Page Designer** to distribute via email, social media, or other channels.

---

## Sharing as a Page

Create a customized, standalone **Page** perfect for sharing outside your website. Whether promoting events, sending calendar links via email, or including them in campaigns, the Page Designer offers flexible branding options and real-time previews to ensure your page reflects your style. You can also group multiple calendars into a unified **Booking Hub** for easier navigation.

- To learn more about designing your Page, please take a look at our article on [**How To Design a Page for Your Booking Calendar**](/human-engagement/booking-calendars/sharing-embedding/how-to-design-page-for-your-booking-calendar).
- To learn about pre-filling your **Page** with guest information, please take a look at [**this article**](/human-engagement/booking-calendars/sharing-embedding/how-to-design-page-for-your-booking-calendar).

## Embed Your Calendar on Your Website

Embedding your **Booking Calendar** allows visitors to schedule appointments without ever leaving your site. This option is ideal for businesses that want to provide a fully integrated experience, letting customers seamlessly book while exploring your services.

For step-by-step instructions on how to embed your **Booking Calendar**, please [**click here**](/human-engagement/booking-calendars/sharing-embedding/how-to-embed-your-booking-calendar-on-your-website).

---

Whether you choose to embed your calendar or share a standalone booking page, both options help you offer a smooth and professional scheduling experience for your customers. Explore deeper customization and setup options by following the linked guides.
