You can use the **Share** option in **Booking Calendar** or **Booking Hub** lobbies to generate a QR code to easily distribute your booking links on physical materials or mobile displays.

---

## Generating the QR Code

Follow these steps to create and download your QR code:

1. Go to **Booking Calendars** or **Booking Hubs** from the left navigation menu.
2. Click **Share** next to the specific Booking link you want to share as a QR code.
3. Click **Download QR code**.

The QR code will automatically download to your device so that you can use it right away.

---

### Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Can I prefill guest details while using a QR code?**

- **A:** Yes, if you prefill the information before clicking **Download QR code**, that information will be prefilled when the guest uses the QR code. See our [**Prefilling Guest Information in Your Booking Calendar**](/human-engagement/booking-calendars/sharing-embedding/prefilling-guest-information-in-your-booking-calendar) article for information on prefilling.

**Q: Can I create QR codes that can only be used once?**

- **A:** Yes, you can select the **One-time use** option in the Share pop-up window before clicking the **Download QR code** button. Please note that the QR code will only be able to be used once in total, not once per individual person.
