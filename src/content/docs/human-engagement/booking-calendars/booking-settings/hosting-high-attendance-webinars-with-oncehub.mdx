While dedicated webinar platforms are built for mass broadcasts, they often feel impersonal, leading to lower engagement and attendance. By using **OnceHub Group Sessions** as your primary scheduling and management platform, you can leverage a **_personal invitation_** strategy that drives higher commitment. This article explains how to use **OnceHub as your registration and notification engine** for your webinar while continuing to use your preferred video conferencing tools.

---

## The Trade-Off: Personalization vs. Analytics

The secret to high attendance with OnceHub isn't just automation; it’s the personal touch. Using OnceHub involves a strategic choice: prioritizing engagement over enterprise-level technical reporting.

- **The Personal Touch:** Unlike generic **_broadcast_&#x20;**&#x69;nvites from standard webinar tools, OnceHub invitations look and feel like a personal one-on-one meeting request. Guests are more likely to attend (or notify you if they can't) because they feel a direct connection to the host.
- **Cost Efficiency:** Traditional webinar platforms often require expensive monthly subscriptions or **per-seat** licenses. By using OnceHub with your existing video conferencing tools, you eliminate the need for a separate webinar license, significantly reducing your overhead.
- **The Strategic Trade-off:** Prioritizing engagement over granular reporting (such as viewer **_drop-off_&#x20;**&#x74;imestamps or **_individual poll-response_** mapping) can **significantly increase attendance**. You still retain critical data, including registration conversion rates, source tracking, and final attendance logs via your video conferencing tool.

---

## Step 1: Configuring Your Group Session

To host a webinar, use the [**Group Session**](/human-engagement/booking-calendars/booking-settings/booking-calendar-group-sessions) feature of OnceHub. Group Sessions allow multiple guests to book seats in the same time slot until the maximum capacity is reached.

- **Set Capacity:** Create a Group Session and define your maximum capacity. Once the limit is reached, the booking link automatically stops accepting new sign-ups.
- **Professional Branding:** Instead of sending a generic link, embed your OnceHub Booking Calendar into a custom landing page. Using no-code vibe coding apps, you can create a high-conversion, branded registration experience that far outperforms standard webinar rooms.
- **Collect Lead Data:** Use **Custom Fields** on your Booking Form to collect specific data (e.g., Job Title, Company Size) to tailor your webinar content and meet your audience's needs.

---

## Step 2: Selecting Your Video Conferencing Tool

OnceHub works as the automated coordinator in the background while you use the video conferencing tools you already know.

- **Native Integrations:** OnceHub connects directly with **Zoom**, **Google Meet**, and **Microsoft Teams**.
- **Flexible Link Options:** Choose between automatically generating a **unique meeting link** for every guest or using a consistent **static link** (like a Personal Meeting Room). In both cases, the link is automatically included in the guest's calendar invitation and confirmation email.

---

## Step 3: Maximize Attendance with Smart Reminders

**No-shows** are the biggest hurdle for any webinar. OnceHub solves this with an **automated multi-channel notification system** to keep your event top-of-mind.

- **Set & Forget Notifications:** Configure up to three automated reminders to ensure guests remain engaged without manual effort.
- **Multi-Channel Reach:** Ensure you combine your Email reminders with an SMS text to catch your guest’s attention. Sending a text 15 minutes before you go live boosts real-time attendance.

---

## Step 4: Owning Your Data

OnceHub automatically adds every registrant to your [**Contact Database**](/contact-management/introduction-to-contacts-in-oncehub) the moment they sign up. This prevents your valuable data from being trapped inside a third-party video conferencing tool. After the webinar, you can transition these engaged contacts into your sales funnel for follow-up.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

**Can I use OnceHub for webinars if I don't have a dedicated webinar software license?**

**A:** Yes. Since OnceHub manages the registration and reminders, you can use the standard version of Zoom, Google Meet, or Microsoft Teams to host the session.

**How does OnceHub prevent overbooking for my webinar?**

**A:** Once you set the **Maximum number of attendees** in your Group Session settings, the booking link will automatically show the slot as **unavailable** once that limit is reached.

**What happens if a guest needs to cancel?**

**A:** OnceHub includes a cancellation link in the confirmation email. If a guest cancels, OnceHub updates your attendance list and instantly re-opens that slot on your booking link for another person to register.

**Will attendees receive a calendar invite automatically?**

**A:** Yes. Upon successful registration, OnceHub sends a calendar invitation to your guest with the meeting link included, ensuring the webinar is blocked on their schedule.

**Can I charge for webinar registration through OnceHub?**

**A:** Yes. If you have a [**payment integration (Stripe)**](/account-integrations/payment-collection/stripe/how-to-connect-oncehub-with-stripe) connected to your OnceHub account, you can require payment at the time of booking.

**Do I need to purchase a separate Webinar plan or add-on?**

**A:** No. Unlike most scheduling platforms that charge extra for webinar features, OnceHub includes **Group Sessions** in the standard **Schedule Plan**. There are no **per-webinar** fees or expensive add-ons required to manage high-attendance events.
