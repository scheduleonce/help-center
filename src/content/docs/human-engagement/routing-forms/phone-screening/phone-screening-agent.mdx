**Phone Screening Agent** is a **no-setup feature** that transforms your existing **Routing Forms** into a conversational, natural-language phone experience.

For information on how to assign a phone number, see our article on [**How to Assign a Phone Number to Your Routing Form**](/human-engagement/routing-forms/phone-screening/how-to-assign-phone-number-to-your-routing-form).

---

## How Phone Screening Works

Each **Routing Form** can be assigned its own **dedicated phone number**. This turns the form into a voice-based experience where callers answer questions and are routed automatically based on the form’s pre-set logic.

For information on how to manage dedicated numbers, see our article on [**Managing Phone Numbers**](/billing/managing-phone-numbers).

## The Guest Experience

When a client calls your dedicated number, they interact with the agents which is designed to handle the conversation:

- [**Personalized Welcome**](/human-engagement/booking-calendars/phone-booking/call-recording-compliance-guidance-for-phone-agent)**:** The assistant greets the caller with your custom welcome message.
- **Conversational Logic:** The assistant asks the questions defined in your Routing Form and follows the specific routing logic of said form.
- **Integrated Booking:** If your form includes a Booking Calendar, the caller can finalize a booking directly during the phone call.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

**Are there extra costs for my callers to use this service?**

**A:** Yes, standard phone call rates apply.

**Are there extra costs for me as a user to use a Phone Screening Agent?**

**A:** Yes, each dedicated phone number requires a [**phone number license**](/billing/managing-your-subscription#adding-or-removing-phone-number-licenses).

**Can I generate phone numbers for any country?**

**A:** Currently, self-serve generation is limited to U.S., Canada and UK phone numbers. For all other countries, please [**contact our Sales team**](https://www.oncehub.com/contact?).
